Assignment 01 is the first practical step after Slide 01,Intelligent System Development . Slide 01

Intelligence → Intelligent System→ Representation → Learning → Decision → Action

Foundation
Central idea
A traditional ML system does not directly operate on the whole real world. It operates on a
computational representation of selected information.
Real-world problem→ Data → Feature representation→ ML model → Prediction
The goal is therefore not merely to “train a classifier.” The goal is to develop and explain a small
intelligent system.

4 Intelligence in This Assignment
In this first assignment, intelligence is deliberately treated in a limited and operational sense. The
system demonstrates a form of intelligence when it can:
• receive information;
• represent relevant information computationally;
• learn a relationship from examples;
• produce a prediction for a new case;
• support a decision or application.
Thus:
Learning from examples+generalization +prediction
is the working notion of intelligence for Assignment 01.
This is not intended to be a complete definition of intelligence. Later assignments will extend
the notion through deep learning, knowledge, retrieval, generation, interaction, and deployment.
5 Data Representation: The Central Idea
A real-world object or event can be represented in different ways. For structured data, an observation
is represented as a feature vector:
xi = [xi1,xi2,...,xid] ∈ Rd.
A supervised dataset is:
D = {(xi,yi)}N
i=1.
Here:
• xi is the input representation;
• d is the number of features;
• yi is the target;
• N is the number of observations.
The complete feature matrix is:
X ∈ RN ×d.
Foundation
Key principle
The model does not receive “the real world.” It receives a representation of selected informa-
tion. Therefore:
Representation determines what information is available to learning.

6 Why Traditional Machine Learning Fits Here
Slide 01 places traditional ML in the following pipeline:
Raw / Domain Data→ Human-designed Features→ Traditional ML Model→ Prediction
This is the central technical focus of Assignment 01.
Students should understand the distinction between:
Traditional ML : human-designed feature representation+learned prediction model
Deep Learning : learned representation+learned prediction model.
The second approach will be studied later.

20 Connection to the History of AI
Slide 01 presents intelligent-system development as an evolving history of representations and learn-
ing mechanisms.
Assignment 01 occupies one important stage:
Structured Data → Feature Engineering→ Traditional ML
Students should briefly explain where their system belongs in the broader evolution:
Symbolic AI → Explicit rules and symbols
Statistical ML → Features + learned models
Deep Learning → Learned representations
Foundation / Generative AI → Large learned representations + generation
Interactive / Agentic Systems → Perception + reasoning + action + interaction.
The purpose is not to study the complete history in Assignment 01. The purpose is to understand
that traditional ML is one stage in the development of intelligent systems.
