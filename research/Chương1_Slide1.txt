--- PAGE 6 ---
What Is Intelligence?
There is no single universally accepted engineering definition.
For this course, we use a functional view:
Observe Represent Learn Reason Decide Act
Intelligence can be viewed through capabilities such as:
▶ acquiring information from the environment;
▶ forming useful internal representations;
▶ learning from data or experience;
▶ reasoning under uncertainty or constraints;
▶ making goal-directed decisions;
▶ acting and adapting from feedback.

--- PAGE 56 ---
Model Persistence
Once a model is trained, it can be saved for later use.
Training→Saved Model→Prediction.
Example:
import pickle
with open("model.pkl", "wb") as f:
pickle.dump(model, f)
Then:
with open("model.pkl", "rb") as f:
model = pickle.load(f)
prediction = model.predict(X_new)
Key Idea
New input must be represented and preprocessed in the same way as the training data.
